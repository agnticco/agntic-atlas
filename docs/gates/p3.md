## Recorded exactness decision (ENGINEERING-LOG.md:85-97)

"Exact" is formally defined as **structural equivalence AND provable
runnability**, not byte-for-byte LLM-output identity. The converger is
non-deterministic by design; requiring identical field values would be brittle
and defeat the purpose of an elicitation engine. This decision is recorded in
ENGINEERING-LOG.md § "The frozen canonical spec" and is a *stronger* bar than byte
comparison because it proves the spec actually executes.
